## Suno

**Para:** support@suno.com (y el formulario de *Business / Partnerships* de su web)
**Asunto:** Crazy rAdIo – 24/7 internet radio for Suno-made songs (Spain)

Hello Suno team,

My name is Jose Luis Besteiro and I am launching **Crazy rAdIo** (czyradio.com), a 24/7 internet radio station from Vigo, Spain, that broadcasts only AI-generated music submitted by its creators.

Before we open to the public, we would like to introduce the project and make sure we work in line with your terms:

- Creators upload their own songs and declare which tool and plan they used.
- We only accept Suno songs created on a **Pro or Premier** subscription and downloaded through Suno's official channels. Songs from the free plan are rejected automatically.
- Each creator confirms they own the song and authorises us to broadcast it and stream it on demand (no downloads). They can withdraw it at any time.
- Songs are credited with the creator's name and "made with Suno".
- We will hold the corresponding music licences in Spain (SGAE / AGEDI / AIE).

We would appreciate it if you could confirm that this use is consistent with your Terms of Service, or let us know about any requirement we should add. We would also be glad to explore a partnership, for example a "Made with Suno" show or featuring Suno creators.

Thank you very much,

Jose Luis Besteiro
Crazy rAdIo – https://czyradio.com
hola@czyradio.com
